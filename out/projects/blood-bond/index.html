Blood Bond

A mobile app that connects blood donors and recipients in real time, using personalised reminders and location-based matching to streamline the donation process.

GitHub

The problem

Finding a compatible donor quickly can be difficult when requests, availability, and location information are disconnected.

The solution

Blood Bond combines real-time donor-recipient connections, location-aware matching, and reminders in a focused mobile experience.
