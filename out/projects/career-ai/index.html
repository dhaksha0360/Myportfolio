Case study · AI-powered career platform

CareerAI

A full-stack platform that recommends the top three suitable IT career roles from a student's academic profile, skills, experience, and preferences.

The problem

Students often have fragmented information about technology career paths and little guidance connecting their individual strengths to specific roles.

The solution

A guided assessment and ML-backed recommendation experience that turns profile signals into ranked roles, confidence indicators, and useful career context.
